# Privacy and Personal Data Requests

## Types of requests
Customers can ask for a copy of their personal data, correction of wrong data, deletion of their data, or to stop marketing use of their data. EU and UK customers have these rights under GDPR and UK GDPR; California residents under the CCPA; Canadian customers under PIPEDA.

## Who handles them
Personal data requests are handled only by the Quillfen Privacy Team, not by general support. Support agents must not export, delete or change personal data on a customer's behalf. Agents pass the request to the Privacy Team and tell the customer they will be contacted by email.

## Timelines and identity checks
The Privacy Team confirms the customer's identity first. Requests are completed within 30 days of identity confirmation (45 days for CCPA requests). Customers can also submit requests at privacy@quillfen.example.

## Suspected data breach or account misuse
Reports of a possible data breach, leaked personal data, or someone else using a customer's identity are urgent. They must be passed to the Privacy Team and Security Team immediately; support must not speculate about the cause.
