# Known issue: Aria Buds left earbud disconnects (firmware 3.2.1)

## Symptoms
After updating Aria Buds to firmware 3.2.1, the left earbud drops out every few minutes while the right one keeps playing. Calls may switch to the right bud only. Re-pairing helps for a short time.

## Cause
A bug in firmware 3.2.1 affects the hand-over of the audio link between the two earbuds.

## Resolution
Update to firmware 3.2.4 in the QuillCloud app > Devices > Aria Buds > Firmware, with both buds in the open case. Then reset the earbuds (hold the case button 15 seconds until amber flashes three times) and pair again. A replacement is not needed.

## Status
Fixed in firmware 3.2.4, released 22 September 2026.
