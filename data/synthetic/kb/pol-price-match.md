# Price Match and Price Drop Policy

## Matching a competitor's price
If you find the identical item (same model, colour and storage) for less at a listed major retailer within 14 days of your purchase, we refund the difference. The item must be new and in stock at that retailer. We do not match marketplace sellers, auction sites, flash or lightning deals, bundle prices, membership-only prices, or coupon-code prices. Submit a request in Account > Orders > Get help > Price match with a link to the offer.

## When our own price drops
If the Quillfen price of an item drops within 14 days of your purchase, we refund the difference on request. Quillfen Plus members have 30 days. Each item can receive one price adjustment only. Price adjustments are paid to the original payment method.
