# Known issue: Order confirmation emails delayed during the Autumn Sale

## Symptoms
During the Autumn Sale, customers do not receive an order confirmation email, or it arrives several hours late. Some place the same order a second time because they think it failed.

## Cause
A very high volume of emails during the sale.

## Resolution
If the order appears in Account > Orders, or the customer received a confirmation screen with an order number, the order is placed and does not need to be repeated. Duplicate orders can be cancelled free of charge until they ship.

## Status
Emails are being sent as the queue clears.
