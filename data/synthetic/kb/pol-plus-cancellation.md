# Cancelling Quillfen Plus

## How to cancel
Go to Account > Membership > Cancel. Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep the benefits until the end of the period you already paid for.

## Monthly plans
Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time. We do not give partial refunds for the current month.

## Annual plans
- Within 14 days of the start or renewal date, and no Plus benefits used: full refund.
- Within 14 days, but benefits were used: refund minus the value of benefits used. Free shipping is valued at the standard shipping price that would have been charged.
- After 14 days: no refund in the US, UK and EU; benefits continue until the end of the year.

In Canada, annual plans can be cancelled at any time for a prorated refund of the unused full months, minus the value of benefits used.

## Membership and earlier orders
Cancelling Plus does not change the return window or warranty of orders placed while you were a member.
