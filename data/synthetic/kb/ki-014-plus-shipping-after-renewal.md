# Known issue: Plus free shipping not applied after renewal

## Symptoms
Quillfen Plus members whose membership renewed in the last 48 hours are charged standard shipping at checkout, even though the membership page shows them as active.

## Cause
After a renewal, the checkout keeps an old membership status for up to 48 hours.

## Resolution
Sign out and sign in again before checking out; this refreshes the membership status. If shipping was already charged, support refunds the shipping charge to the original payment method on request; no need to cancel the order.

## Status
Fix in progress.
