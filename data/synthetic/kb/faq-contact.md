# Contacting Quillfen Support

## Channels and hours
Chat in the QuillCloud app or on quillfen.example, Monday to Friday 08:00-20:00 and Saturday 09:00-17:00 local time. Email support@quillfen.example at any time; we reply within 1 business day. Quillfen Plus members have a priority chat queue.

## Speaking to a person
Our assistant handles common questions. Type "agent" in chat at any time to be transferred to a human agent during opening hours. Outside opening hours, leave a message and an agent will reply by email.

## Feedback and complaints
Send product feedback or reviews from the product page. Formal complaints go through Get help > Make a complaint; a supervisor replies within 5 business days.
