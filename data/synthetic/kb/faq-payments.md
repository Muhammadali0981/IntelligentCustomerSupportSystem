# Payments FAQ

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Quillfen gift cards in all regions. Klarna pay-in-4 is available in the US, UK and EU. Interac debit is available in Canada.

## Pending charges and authorisations
When you place an order we place a temporary authorisation on your card. If a payment attempt fails and you try again, your bank may briefly show more than one pending authorisation. Only the successful one is charged; the others disappear, usually within 3-7 business days depending on the bank.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails, check the billing address matches your card statement and that 3-D Secure verification was completed. Then try again or use another method. We cannot see why a bank declined a card; only your bank can tell you.

## Currency
Each regional store charges in its own currency (USD, CAD, GBP or EUR). Your bank may add a foreign transaction fee if your card is in another currency.
