# Warranty Policy

## Coverage periods
Quillfen-brand products (Aria, Nimbus, Hearth, Lumen and Volt) carry a limited warranty against manufacturing defects:
- US and Canada: 1 year from delivery
- UK and EU: 2 years from delivery (legal guarantee)

Quillfen Plus members get one extra year on top of these periods for products bought while the membership was active. Products from other brands are covered by their manufacturer's warranty; we help you contact them.

## What is not covered
Accidental damage (drops, cracked screens), liquid damage beyond the product's rating, unauthorised repairs, and normal cosmetic wear. Aria Buds are rated IPX4: sweat and rain are fine, but submersion in water is liquid damage. Batteries are covered if capacity falls below 80% within the warranty period; normal gradual battery wear above 80% is not a defect.

## How to make a claim
Open Account > Orders > Get help > Warranty claim and enter the serial number (on the box, or in the QuillCloud app under Device info). We first run remote troubleshooting. If the product is faulty we repair or replace it; we decide which. Replacement units ship after we receive the faulty one. Quillfen Plus members get advance replacement: the new unit ships first and you return the old one within 14 days.
