# Shipping Rates and Delivery Options

## Free shipping thresholds
From 1 September 2026, standard shipping is free for orders at or above:
- US: $75
- Canada: CA$99
- UK: £60
- EU: €70

Below the threshold, standard shipping costs $6.99 / CA$9.99 / £4.99 / €5.99. Quillfen Plus members get free standard shipping on every order with no minimum. This policy replaces all earlier shipping FAQs and rate tables.

## Express shipping
Express shipping costs $14.99 / CA$19.99 / £9.99 / €12.99. Quillfen Plus members in the US and Canada get free express shipping on orders over $150 / CA$200. Express is not available to PO boxes or to remote areas flagged at checkout.

## Delivery times
Business days after the order ships:
- US: standard 3-5, express 1-2
- Canada: standard 4-8, express 2-3
- UK: standard 2-4, express next business day if ordered before 14:00 UK time
- EU: standard 4-7, express 2-3

Orders usually ship within 1 business day. Large sale events can add 1-2 days.

## Where we ship from
US and Canadian orders ship from our US warehouse; duties for Canada are included in the price. UK orders ship from the UK warehouse. EU orders normally ship from the Netherlands warehouse. Each regional store only ships to addresses in its own region.
