# Known issue: EU customers charged customs fees on parcels from the UK warehouse

## Symptoms
EU customers who ordered Hearth or Lumen products since 1 October 2026 are asked by the carrier to pay import VAT or a customs clearance fee before delivery. The parcel comes from the UK.

## Cause
Some parcels from the temporary UK warehouse were sent without the "duty paid" label.

## Resolution
Customers should pay the fee to receive the parcel, keep the carrier's receipt, and submit a customs refund claim in Account > Orders > Get help > Customs fee within 60 days of delivery. The full amount is refunded to the original payment method within 5 business days of approval. Customers may also refuse the parcel; it is then returned and the order is refunded in full.

## Status
Labelling corrected for new shipments from 16 October 2026.
