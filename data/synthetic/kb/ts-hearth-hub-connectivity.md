# Hearth Hub Wi-Fi and Connectivity

## Network requirements
The Hearth Hub connects only to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi networks. It does not support 5 GHz. If your router combines both bands under one name, the hub picks 2.4 GHz automatically. Captive-portal networks (hotels, dorms with a login page) are not supported.

## Hub shows offline
Check the hub's light: solid white is online, pulsing orange means no Wi-Fi, solid orange means Wi-Fi works but no internet. Restart the hub by unplugging it for 10 seconds. Make sure the router is within about 10 metres or two walls.

## Changing your Wi-Fi network or password
In the QuillCloud app go to Devices > Hearth Hub > Wi-Fi > Change network. The hub must be online to change networks this way. If it is already offline, hold the hub's button for 5 seconds until the light pulses blue and set it up again.

## Factory reset
Hold the button for 20 seconds until the light flashes red. All paired devices and automations are removed and must be added again.
