# Nimbus 14 Display and External Monitors

## Screen flicker
Update the graphics driver in Quillfen Update Utility > Drivers. Turn off "Adaptive brightness" in Display settings. If flicker also appears in the BIOS setup screen (press F2 at start-up), it is likely a hardware fault; make a warranty claim.

## Connecting an external monitor
Only the left USB-C port supports video output (DisplayPort Alt Mode), for one external monitor up to 4K at 60 Hz. The right USB-C port is for charging and data only. Use a USB-C to HDMI or DisplayPort adapter, or a USB-C monitor.

## Wrong resolution or blurry text
Set the resolution to the recommended value in Display settings and the scale to 150% for the built-in screen. For an external monitor, check the cable supports its full resolution.
