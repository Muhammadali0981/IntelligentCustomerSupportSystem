# QuillCloud App Troubleshooting

## Updating the app
Get the latest version from the App Store or Google Play. The installed version is shown in the app > Settings > About. Device firmware updates also need the latest app.

## Clearing the app cache
On Android: Settings > Apps > QuillCloud > Storage > Clear cache. On iPhone, delete and reinstall the app. Your account data and devices are stored online and are not lost.

## Cloud backup
Quillfen Plus members can back up photos and files to QuillCloud (200 GB). Turn it on in the app > Backup. Backup is not available in Canada until 2027.

## Wallet and gift cards in the app
The app's Wallet shows saved payment methods and gift cards added to your account.
