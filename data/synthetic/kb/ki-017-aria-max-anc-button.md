# Known issue: Aria Max ANC button stops working after firmware 2.0

## Symptoms
After the update to firmware 2.0, pressing the ANC button on Aria Max does nothing. Noise cancelling can still be changed in the QuillCloud app. Other buttons work.

## Cause
Firmware 2.0 does not always migrate the old button settings correctly.

## Resolution
Factory reset the headphones: with them on, hold the power and ANC buttons together for 15 seconds until the light flashes red and white, then pair again. The button works normally afterwards. No repair is needed.

## Status
Fixed for new updates in firmware 2.0.1.
