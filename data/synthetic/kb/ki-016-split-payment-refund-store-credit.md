# Known issue: Split-payment refunds issued fully as store credit

## Symptoms
Customers who paid with a gift card plus a card or PayPal find that their whole refund was added as store credit, instead of the card or PayPal share going back to the original method.

## Cause
A refund system error affecting returns of split-payment orders processed since 20 September 2026.

## Resolution
Support can convert the card or PayPal share of the store credit back to the original payment method on request, as long as the store credit has not been spent. It then arrives in 5-10 business days for cards or 3-5 for PayPal. The gift card share stays as gift card balance.

## Status
Fix in progress.
