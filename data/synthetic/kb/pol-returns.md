# Returns Policy

## Standard return window
You can return most items bought from Quillfen Electronics within 30 days of the delivery date for any reason. Quillfen Plus members get a 60-day return window on every order placed while their membership was active. The item must be in its original packaging with all accessories, manuals and cables. Start a return in Account > Orders > Return item; you will receive a prepaid label and a QR code.

Items sold by marketplace sellers (shown as "Sold by" another company on the product page) follow that seller's own return policy, not this one.

## Holiday returns
Items delivered between 15 November and 31 December can be returned until 31 January of the following year. If your normal window (30 or 60 days) ends later than 31 January, the later date applies.

## EU and UK right of withdrawal
Customers in the EU and UK also have a legal 14-day right of withdrawal, counted from the day of delivery. Within these 14 days you may return an item even if it has been opened and tried, and no restocking fee is charged. This right exists in addition to the 30-day policy above; it does not shorten it.

## Items that cannot be returned
- Aria Buds and other in-ear products once the hygiene seal on the ear tips is broken, unless the product is faulty. This applies in every region, including during the EU/UK 14-day withdrawal period.
- Software, game and gift codes once the code has been revealed.
- Gift cards (see the Gift Card Policy).

Faulty items are always covered by the Warranty Policy instead.

## Return shipping and restocking fees
Returns of faulty or wrongly shipped items are free. For change-of-mind returns a label fee is deducted from the refund: $5.99 (US), CA$7.99 (Canada), £4.99 (UK) or €4.99 (EU). Quillfen Plus members never pay the label fee.

Opened Nimbus laptops returned for change of mind more than 14 days after delivery carry a 15% restocking fee. The restocking fee never applies to faulty units, to unopened laptops, or to EU/UK returns made within the 14-day withdrawal period.
