# Aria Max Headphones Troubleshooting

## Noise cancelling modes
Press the ANC button to cycle through Noise cancelling, Transparency and Off. The mode can also be set in the QuillCloud app. Noise cancelling works best with a good seal; check the ear cushions are seated correctly.

## Factory reset
Turn the headphones on. Hold the power button and the ANC button together for 15 seconds until the light flashes red and white. The headphones restart with factory settings, including button settings, and need to be paired again.

## Multipoint connection
Aria Max can connect to two devices at once. Turn on Multipoint in the QuillCloud app > Devices > Aria Max > Connections. Audio plays from the device that started playing last.

## Battery and charging
A full charge gives about 35 hours with noise cancelling on. Use a USB-C cable; a 10-minute charge gives about 4 hours. If the headphones do not turn on, charge for 30 minutes before trying again.
