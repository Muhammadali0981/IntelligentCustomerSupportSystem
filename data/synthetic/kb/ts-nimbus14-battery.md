# Nimbus 14 Battery and Power

## Normal battery life
The Nimbus 14 lasts up to 12 hours of web browsing at 50% brightness. In sleep, it should lose no more than about 1-2% per hour. Heavy tasks, high brightness and many browser tabs shorten battery life.

## Checking battery health
Open Quillfen Control Center > Battery > Health report. It shows the current full-charge capacity as a percentage of the design capacity. Below 80% within the warranty period is covered by the Warranty Policy.

## Updating the BIOS
Open Quillfen Update Utility > BIOS. Connect the charger and keep the laptop plugged in during the update; do not close the lid. The laptop restarts once or twice. The current BIOS version is shown in Quillfen Control Center > System.

## Sleep and hibernate
Sleep keeps the session in memory and uses a little power. Hibernate saves the session to disk and uses none. To hibernate after 3 hours of sleep, set it in Quillfen Control Center > Power > Sleep options.

## Laptop does not charge
Use the included 65 W charger or another USB-C Power Delivery charger of at least 45 W. Try both USB-C ports. If the charging light does not turn on, hold the power button for 20 seconds with the charger disconnected, then connect it again.
