# Known issue: Invoice PDF opens blank in Safari

## Symptoms
Customers using Safari on Mac, iPhone or iPad get a blank white page or an empty PDF when they click Invoice in Account > Orders.

## Cause
The invoice viewer is not compatible with Safari's built-in PDF preview.

## Resolution
Use "Email invoice" on the order page to receive the PDF by email, or open the order page in another browser such as Chrome, Firefox or Edge. The invoice itself is correct.

## Status
Fix in progress.
