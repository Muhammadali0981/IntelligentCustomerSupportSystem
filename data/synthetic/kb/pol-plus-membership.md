# Quillfen Plus Membership

## Prices
Quillfen Plus costs $9.99/month or $99/year (US), CA$12.99/month or CA$129/year (Canada), £8.99/month or £89/year (UK), €9.99/month or €99/year (EU). Membership renews automatically. For annual plans we email a reminder 7 days before renewal.

## Benefits
- Free standard shipping on every order, and free express shipping over $150 / CA$200 (US and Canada only)
- 60-day returns and no return label fees
- One extra year of warranty and advance replacement on Quillfen-brand products
- 5% member price on Quillfen-brand products
- QuillCloud backup with 200 GB of storage
- 30 days of Lumen Cam cloud recordings (instead of 7)

## Regional limits
QuillCloud backup is not available to members in Canada until 2027 because of data residency requirements; Canadian members get the other benefits. Free express shipping applies only in the US and Canada.

## Member price and promo codes
The 5% member price does not combine with promo codes. At checkout we apply whichever gives the larger discount.
