# Lumen Cam Troubleshooting

## Setting up the camera
Add the camera in the QuillCloud app > Devices > Add device > Lumen Cam and scan the QR code on its base. Like the Hearth Hub, Lumen Cam needs a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network.

## Night vision and image flicker
Night vision switches on automatically in low light. Lines or flicker in the image under indoor lights are caused by the mains frequency. Set Anti-flicker to 50 Hz (UK, EU) or 60 Hz (US, Canada) in the app > Lumen Cam > Video.

## Cloud recordings
Motion clips are stored in the cloud for 7 days free of charge, or 30 days with Quillfen Plus. Clips can also be saved to a microSD card of up to 256 GB.

## Camera offline
Unplug the camera for 10 seconds. If it stays offline, hold the reset button for 10 seconds and add it again.
