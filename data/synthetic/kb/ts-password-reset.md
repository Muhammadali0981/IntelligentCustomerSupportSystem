# Resetting Your Password

## Requesting a reset link
Choose "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and enter your account email. We send a reset link from noreply@quillfen.example. The link is valid for 60 minutes, and only the most recent link works.

## Reset email not received
Check the spam or junk folder and search for noreply@quillfen.example. Make sure you used the email address of the account and the correct regional store. Wait a few minutes before requesting another link, because each new request makes earlier links invalid.

## No access to the email address
If you can no longer access the account's email address, support must verify your identity before changing it. This takes 2-3 business days.
