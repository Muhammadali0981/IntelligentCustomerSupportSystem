# Aria Buds Troubleshooting

## Pairing with a phone or laptop
Put both earbuds in the case, keep the lid open and hold the case button for 3 seconds until the light flashes white. Select "Aria Buds" in your device's Bluetooth settings. Aria Buds remember up to 8 devices but connect to one at a time.

## Resetting the earbuds
Remove "Aria Buds" from your device's Bluetooth list. Put both buds in the case, close the lid for 10 seconds, open it, and hold the case button for 15 seconds until the light flashes amber three times. Pair again as new.

## Updating firmware
Open the QuillCloud app > Devices > Aria Buds > Firmware. Keep the buds in the open case with at least 50% battery during the update. The current firmware version is shown on the same screen.

## One earbud not charging
Clean the metal contacts on the earbud and inside the case with a dry cotton swab. Make sure the ear tip is not stopping the bud from sitting flat. If the case light does not show amber when the bud is inserted, run a warranty claim.

## Sound or microphone problems
Check the earbuds are selected as the audio output and input on your device. Turn off and on "Ambient mode" in the app. Low volume on one side is often caused by earwax on the mesh; clean it gently with a dry soft brush.
