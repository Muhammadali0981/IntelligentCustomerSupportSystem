# Account Security

## Failed sign-in attempts
After 5 failed sign-in attempts the account is locked for 30 minutes. After 10 failed attempts the account stays locked until the password is reset through the email link.

## Two-step verification
Customers can protect their account with two-step verification using an authenticator app or SMS codes. When it is turned on, we show 10 one-time backup codes. Keep them somewhere safe; each works once.

## What support will never ask for
Quillfen staff never ask for your password, your two-step verification codes, or your full card number, by email, chat or phone. If someone asks for these, do not share them and report the message to security@quillfen.example.

## Suspicious activity
If you see orders you did not place or changes you did not make, reset your password at once and contact support. Support will lock the account and pass the case to the Security Team.
