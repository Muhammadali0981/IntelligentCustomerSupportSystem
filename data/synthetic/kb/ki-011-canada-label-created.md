# Known issue: Canadian orders stuck at "Label created"

## Symptoms
Orders to Canada shipped with NorthPost Express show "Label created" in tracking for 5 days or more, with no further updates. The order page says "Shipped".

## Cause
NorthPost Express is not scanning parcels at pick-up during a backlog at its border hub. Parcels are moving even though tracking does not update.

## Resolution
Most parcels show a scan when they reach the Canadian sorting centre and arrive within 10 business days of shipping. Ask the customer to wait until 15 business days after shipping (the Canadian lost-parcel limit) before we open a lost-parcel investigation. Do not send a replacement earlier.

## Status
Carrier backlog expected to clear by mid-November 2026.
