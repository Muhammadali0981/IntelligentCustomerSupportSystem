# Delivery and Tracking FAQ

## Tracking your order
When your order ships we email a tracking number and link. You can also find it in Account > Orders. The status "Label created" means the carrier has not scanned the parcel yet; it usually changes within 24-48 hours.

## Late or lost parcels
If tracking has not changed for 10 business days (15 business days in Canada), the parcel may be lost. Open Account > Orders > Get help > Parcel not arrived and we will start a carrier investigation and send a replacement or a refund.

## Marked as delivered but not received
Wait 48 hours, because carriers sometimes mark parcels delivered early. Check with neighbours and your building's mail room, and look for a delivery photo in the tracking page. If it still has not arrived, report it in Get help > Parcel not arrived.

## Signature and safe places
Orders over $500 / CA$650 / £400 / €450 need a signature. For other orders you can choose a safe place on the carrier's website.

## Damaged parcels
If the parcel arrives damaged, take photos of the box and item and report it within 7 days in Get help > Damaged item. We send a replacement and a free return label.
