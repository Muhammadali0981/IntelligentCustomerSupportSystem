# Account FAQ

## Creating an account
Sign up at quillfen.example or in the QuillCloud app with an email address and password. Each email address can have one account per regional store.

## Changing your email or details
Change your name, phone number and addresses in Account > Profile. To change your email address we send a confirmation link to both the old and the new address; the change completes when both are confirmed. If you no longer have access to the old address, contact support for an identity check.

## Personal and business accounts
You can switch a personal account to a Quillfen for Business account in Account > Profile > Account type. Business accounts can add a VAT number and several users. Orders stay in the account when you switch.

## Deleting your account
Request deletion in Account > Profile > Delete account. You must first cancel Quillfen Plus and wait until open orders and returns are completed. After the request there is a 30-day grace period in which you can sign in to cancel the deletion. Order records needed for tax law are kept for the legally required time.
