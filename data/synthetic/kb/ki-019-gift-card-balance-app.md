# Known issue: Gift card balance shows 0 in the app Wallet

## Symptoms
Gift cards added to an account show a balance of 0.00 in the QuillCloud app Wallet, although they have not been used.

## Cause
A display error in the app Wallet. The real balance is unchanged and can be used at checkout.

## Resolution
Check the balance on the website in Account > Wallet or at quillfen.example/giftcard. The gift card can be used normally at checkout on the website or in the app.

## Status
Fix planned for the next app release.
