# Hearth Hub Automations and Schedules

## Automations do not run
Check that the hub is online and that every device in the automation shows "Connected". Automations run on the hub itself, so they keep working without internet once saved, but changes need internet to sync.

## Time zone and schedules
Schedules use the hub's time zone, set in the QuillCloud app > Devices > Hearth Hub > Time zone. "Set automatically" uses your phone's location when you set up the hub.

## Devices not responding to the hub
Bring the device closer to the hub, replace batteries in battery-powered sensors, and remove and re-add the device. The hub supports up to 64 devices.
