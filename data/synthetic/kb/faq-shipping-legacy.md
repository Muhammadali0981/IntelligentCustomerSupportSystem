# Shipping FAQ (2025)

## Is shipping free?
Yes! Standard shipping is free on all orders over $50 in the US (CA$65 in Canada, £40 in the UK, €45 in the EU). Smaller orders pay a flat $5.99.

## How fast is delivery?
Most orders arrive within 3-5 business days. EU orders arrive within 3-5 business days from our Netherlands warehouse.

## Do you ship internationally?
We ship to the US, Canada, the UK and the EU. You can order from any regional store and we ship to any of these regions.
