# Known issue: Two-step SMS codes not arriving on some UK networks

## Symptoms
UK customers with two-step verification by SMS do not receive the code, or it arrives after it has expired. This affects some UK mobile networks since mid-October 2026.

## Cause
A delivery problem between our SMS provider and some UK mobile networks.

## Resolution
Sign in with a backup code, or choose "Send code by email instead" on the verification screen. After signing in, we recommend switching two-step verification to an authenticator app in Account > Security.

## Status
Under investigation with the SMS provider.
