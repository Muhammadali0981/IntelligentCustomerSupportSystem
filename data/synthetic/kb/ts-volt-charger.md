# Volt Chargers Troubleshooting

## Charger models
The Volt 30 W charger has one USB-C port. The Volt 65 W charger has two USB-C ports and one USB-A port; when several devices charge at once the 65 W is shared between them.

## Light codes
Solid green: charging. Blinking green: fast charging. Blinking red: overheating or a faulty cable; unplug for 1 minute and try another cable.

## Slow charging
Fast charging needs a cable rated for the power. Laptops need a USB-C cable rated 5 A (100 W or more) for charging above 60 W. Charging slows when the charger is shared between ports.
