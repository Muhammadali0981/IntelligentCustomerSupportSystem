# Sign-in Problems

## Wrong password or account locked
Check the email address and keyboard language. After 5 failed attempts the account locks for 30 minutes; after 10, it stays locked until you reset your password (see Account Security).

## Account belongs to another regional store
Accounts are per regional store. If you created your account on the UK store, you cannot sign in on the US store with the same login. Choose your store at the bottom of the page.

## Signed out repeatedly
Make sure your browser allows cookies from quillfen.example, and that the date and time on your device are correct. In the app, update to the latest version.
