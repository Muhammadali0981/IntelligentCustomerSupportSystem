# Invoices FAQ

## Getting an invoice
An invoice is created when your order ships. Download it as a PDF in Account > Orders > Invoice, or choose "Email invoice" to receive it by email. Guest customers get the invoice by email only.

## VAT invoices for businesses
UK and EU business customers can get a VAT invoice by entering their VAT number at checkout. A VAT number cannot be added to an invoice that has already been issued, except through a correction request to the billing team within 30 days of the invoice date.

## Changing invoice details
The name and address on an invoice are taken from the billing details at checkout. Changes after the invoice is issued are only possible through a correction request within 30 days.
