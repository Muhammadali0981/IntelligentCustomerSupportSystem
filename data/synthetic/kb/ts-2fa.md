# Two-step Verification Problems

## Code not accepted
Codes from an authenticator app change every 30 seconds. Enter the newest code. If codes are always rejected, check that your phone's time is set automatically.

## SMS code not arriving
Check your phone has signal and that the number in Account > Security is correct. Wait 60 seconds before requesting another code.

## Lost phone or no access to codes
Use one of your backup codes to sign in, then set up two-step verification again on your new phone. Without backup codes, support must verify your identity before turning off two-step verification; this takes 2-3 business days and cannot be skipped.
