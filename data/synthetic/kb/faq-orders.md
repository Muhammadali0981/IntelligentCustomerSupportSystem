# Orders FAQ

## Placing an order
Add items to your cart and check out with a Quillfen account or as a guest. Guest orders can be tracked with the order number and email address, but returns and warranty claims are easier with an account.

## Changing an order
You can change the shipping address, quantities or items within 1 hour of placing the order in Account > Orders > Edit order. After 1 hour the order goes to the warehouse and cannot be edited; you can cancel it if it has not shipped, or return items after delivery.

## Cancelling an order
Orders can be cancelled free of charge until their status is "Shipped". Cancel in Account > Orders > Cancel order. Once shipped, refuse the delivery or return the item instead.

## Changing the address after shipping
After an order has shipped we cannot change the address. In the US you can ask the carrier to redirect the parcel on their website using the tracking number. In other regions, contact the carrier directly.

## Combining orders
Separate orders cannot be combined into one shipment.
