# Refunds Policy

## When refunds are issued
We issue your refund within 2 business days after the warehouse receives and checks your return. For cancelled orders that had not shipped, the refund is issued immediately. You get an email when the refund is issued, and the order page shows the refund date.

## How long refunds take to arrive
Refunds go back to the original payment method. After we issue them, the time until you see the money depends on your bank or provider:
- Credit and debit cards: 5-10 business days
- PayPal: 3-5 business days
- Klarna: the instalment plan is adjusted within 3 business days
- Quillfen gift card or store credit: instantly

If a card refund has not appeared after 10 business days, ask your bank to trace it using the refund reference number (ARN), which support can provide.

## Split payments
If you paid with more than one method (for example part gift card, part card), the refund is split in the same proportion: the gift card share goes back to the gift card and the rest to the card. Gift card amounts are never refunded as cash.

## What is refunded
The full item price is refunded. Original shipping charges are refunded only when the item was faulty, damaged in transit, or the wrong item was sent. Change-of-mind returns have the return label fee and any restocking fee deducted (see the Returns Policy). Missing accessories may lead to a partial refund.
