# Gift Card Policy

## Using gift cards
Quillfen gift cards can be used on the regional store where they were bought, for any product except other gift cards. Several gift cards can be combined in one order, together with one other payment method.

## Expiry
Gift cards do not expire in the US, Canada and the UK. Gift cards bought in the EU store expire 3 years after purchase.

## Refunds and replacements
Gift cards cannot be returned, refunded or exchanged for cash, except where local law requires it. A lost or stolen gift card can only be replaced if you have the purchase receipt and the remaining balance has not been spent.

## Checking your balance
Check the balance at quillfen.example/giftcard or in Account > Wallet. Balances of gift cards added to your account show in the Wallet.
