# Customs, Duties and Taxes

## Prices include duties
All prices in the Canadian, UK and EU stores include import duties and taxes (VAT or GST/HST). Shipments are sent "delivered duty paid": Quillfen pays any customs charges. Customers should never be asked by a carrier to pay customs fees on a Quillfen order.

## Shipments from another warehouse
From 1 October 2026, some EU orders for Hearth and Lumen products ship from the UK warehouse while the Netherlands warehouse is expanded. These parcels still travel duty paid and the delivery time stays 4-7 business days.

## If you were charged customs fees
If a carrier charged you duties, VAT or a clearance fee on a Quillfen order, keep the receipt and submit a customs refund claim in Account > Orders > Get help > Customs fee within 60 days of delivery. We refund the full amount to your original payment method within 5 business days of approval.
